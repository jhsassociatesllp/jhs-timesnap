> - **EDF Waiver (GR Waiver) Issuance:** Customer Request Letter must include: Currency & Amount, Beneficiary Details, FEMA Declaration, Purpose of GR Waiver Issuance, Declaration for re-import within 6 months (if applicable), Declaration by exporter that value is not more than five lakh rupees in case of GIFT, Disposal method of the Goods, and Signature Verification.

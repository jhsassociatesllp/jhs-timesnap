Employee Declaration and Undertaking

Code of Conduct, Confidentiality, Data Protection and Compliance Declaration

This Declaration and Undertaking (this "Declaration") is made and executed voluntarily by the undersigned employee (the "Employee") in favour of JHS (the "Organisation"), its affiliates, clients and representatives, as a condition of, and in consideration of, the Employee’s employment or continued employment with the Organisation.

I, the undersigned, being an employee of the Organisation, do hereby solemnly and sincerely declare, affirm and undertake as follows:

1. DECLARATION AS TO ACCURACY OF INFORMATION

1.1 I declare that all information, particulars, documents, certificates, identification proofs, educational qualifications, experience letters and other details submitted by me to the Organisation, whether at the time of my application for employment or at any time during the course of my employment, are true, genuine, accurate and complete to the best of my knowledge, information and belief, and that no material fact has been withheld, suppressed or misrepresented.

1.2 I acknowledge and agree that any discrepancy, falsification, forgery, concealment of facts, or submission of misleading, incomplete or fraudulent information, whether discovered before or after my joining the Organisation, shall render me liable to immediate termination of employment without notice or compensation, in addition to such civil and/or criminal legal action as the Organisation may deem fit and as may be permissible under applicable law.

2. WORKPLACE CONDUCT, RECORDING AND MEDIA RESTRICTIONS

2.1 I shall not record, capture or create any reels, videos, or other social media content within the office premises, at client premises, or in connection with the Organisation’s business, whether using personal or Organisation-owned devices.

2.2 I shall not take, publish, or distribute any photographs within the office premises or at client premises without the prior written authorization of the Organisation.

2.3 I shall not record any meetings, telephone or video calls, screens, or conversations conducted by or on behalf of the Organisation without proper prior authorization from the Organisation.

3. PROHIBITED SUBSTANCES AND CONDUCT ON PREMISES

3.1 I shall not consume, possess, distribute, or be under the influence of any drugs, narcotics, or other prohibited substances within the Organisation’s premises, at client premises, or while representing the Organisation in any capacity.

3.2 I shall not smoke, vape, or use tobacco products of any kind within the office premises or at client premises.

3.3 I shall not play mobile games, cards, chess, or any other recreational games whether on personal or Organisation-owned devices within the office premises or at client premises during working hours.

3.4 I shall not use earphones, headphones, or similar devices to listen to music, videos, or other content for personal, non-work-related purposes during working hours, except with the prior permission of my reporting manager.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROTECTION OF INFORMATION

4.1 I shall hold in strict confidence, and shall not disclose, share, publish or part with, any confidential information belonging to the Company or its clients, including but not limited to client data, internal communications, source code, reports, financial information, business strategies, passwords, and any other proprietary or confidential information (collectively, "Confidential Information"), to any unauthorized person.

4.2 I shall not share, upload, or transmit any Confidential Information via WhatsApp, personal e-mail accounts, social media platforms, personal or unauthorized cloud storage, or any other unauthorized medium. I shall also refrain from using WhatsApp or other personal messaging platforms to circulate Organisation documents or files for routine work purposes, and shall use only official/authorised channels for such sharing.

4.3 I shall not copy, forward, download, print, photograph or otherwise circulate any office documents, client information, screenshots, recordings, or Organisation materials without proper authorization.

4.4 I shall maintain, and shall continue to maintain, the confidentiality of all Confidential Information both during the term of my employment and at all times thereafter, without limit of time, save and except where disclosure is required by law or by a competent court or regulatory authority.

5. USE OF ORGANISATION ASSETS, SYSTEMS AND ACCESS CREDENTIALS

5.1 I shall not misuse, misappropriate, or effect any theft or unauthorized removal of Organisation assets, equipment, documents, or data, whether tangible or intangible.

5.2 I shall not install any unauthorized software, applications, or devices on Organisation-owned systems or networks.

5.3 I shall not share my access credentials, passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), or Organisation accounts with any other person, whether internal or external to the Organisation.

5.4 I shall use Organisation-issued devices, systems, e-mail accounts, and internet services solely for authorized business purposes and in accordance with the Organisation’s IT and acceptable-use policies.

6. SECURITY, SAFETY AND REPORTING OBLIGATIONS

6.1 I shall comply with all security, safety, and compliance policies of the Organisation, as amended and communicated to me from time to time.

6.2 I shall immediately report to management any security incident, data breach, theft, policy violation, or suspicious activity that comes to my knowledge or attention.

7. WORKPLACE BEHAVIOUR AND ANTI-HARASSMENT

7.1 I shall conduct myself with professionalism, courtesy and respect towards all employees, clients, and other stakeholders of the Organisation, and shall not engage in any act of harassment, discrimination, violence, misconduct, or other inappropriate behaviour within the workplace.

7.2 I acknowledge that any breach of this Clause 7 shall attract disciplinary action in accordance with the Organisation’s applicable policies and prevailing law.

8. CONSEQUENCES OF BREACH

8.1 I acknowledge and agree that any breach of this Declaration, the Organisation’s Code of Conduct, my confidentiality obligations, or any applicable legal requirements, may result in disciplinary action against me, up to and including termination of my employment, without prejudice to any other right or remedy available to the Organisation under applicable law, including recovery of damages and/or injunctive relief.

8.2 I understand that the obligations undertaken by me under this Declaration are in addition to, and not in derogation of, any obligations imposed upon me under my employment agreement/appointment letter, the Organisation’s policies, or applicable law.

9. RETURN OF ORGANISATION PROPERTY ON SEPARATION

9.1 Upon separation from the Organisation, howsoever arising, I shall promptly return to the Organisation all of its assets, documents, access cards, equipment, devices, and Confidential Information in my possession or control, and shall not retain any copies, extracts or reproductions thereof in any form.

10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Severability: If any provision of this Declaration is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable under applicable law, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and shall be construed so as to give effect to the intent of this Declaration to the maximum extent permitted by law.

10.2 Governing Law and Jurisdiction: This Declaration shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, and shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts at the place of the Organisation’s registered/branch office where the Employee is engaged.

10.3 Entire Undertaking: This Declaration constitutes a binding undertaking given by me to the Organisation and shall be read together with, and shall not derogate from, the terms of my appointment letter, employment agreement, and the Organisation’s policies, as may be amended from time to time.

11. ANTI-BRIBERY, GIFTS AND FAVOURS

11.1 I shall not offer, give, solicit, or accept any bribe, kickback, illegal gratification, or improper payment, whether in cash or in kind, to or from any client, vendor, government official, or other third party in connection with the Organisation’s business.

11.2 I shall not accept any gift, favour, hospitality, or other benefit of significant value from clients, vendors, or other stakeholders that could reasonably be perceived to influence my professional judgment, without prior disclosure to and approval of management.

I shall neither use (directly or indirectly) nor disclose such Confidential Information nor permit (intentionally or otherwise) such Confidential Information to be used or disclosed, except with the prior written consent of the Firm. I will also maintain confidentiality in respect of all details and matters related to my compensation and will not share such information with others (including other employees of the Firm) unless specifically requested by the relevant authority in the Firm. I shall not misuse or personally carry on any medium client information made available to me for audit purposes. All client material shall be returned by me on completion of client work or expiry of client contract, whichever is earlier. I fully understand that any breach of this condition will result in legal notice and consequential legal action pursued by the Firm or clients as the case may be, and I shall be responsible to indemnify the Firm against my negligence.

If during the course of the performance of my duties, I make or discover jointly or individually any intellectual property (the "Works For Hire"), I shall disclose and assign to the Firm full details of such intellectual property rights (including all present and future copyright and copyright revisions and extensions) together with the right to apply for and hold any trademarks in respect of such intellectual property rights which assignment shall take effect upon the creation of each of the intellectual property rights. I will assign to the Firm (including present assignment of future rights) absolutely and free from all encumbrances all my rights including moral rights, title, and interest in any and all intellectual property rights together with all accrued rights of action in respect of any infringement of such intellectual property rights.

Conduct and Discipline: I shall at all times render high standards of services, ensure proper care, and exercise independent professional judgment. I shall not, directly or indirectly, make any statement or become privy to any act, omission, practice or competition which is harmful or likely to be harmful to the interest of the Firm. During my term with the organization, I will be governed by the rules and regulations with respect to conduct and discipline and other matters as may be framed by the Firm from time to time. I shall comply with the "Code of Conduct" and the Firm's Policy and confirm periodically as and when required, the policy/procedures spelt out in the "Code of Conduct" and the Firm's Policy.

I declare that I have read and understood the Information Security Guidelines and shall abide by them under all circumstances.

I declare that there are no pending civil and/or criminal legal proceedings/notices or disciplinary proceedings from ICAI or FIRs registered against me or my immediate family members.

I declare that I shall not abscond from my daily responsibilities and in the event I don't report without due intimation, then I shall compensate and indemnify the firm as per the discretion of the management.

I declare that I am not conflicted in continuing as an employee/partner/consultant in the Firm and shall disclose any other business interests in my annual or need-based declaration(s) to the Firm.

I declare that I am not associated with any activity, group, social media connect that are advocating radical or terrorist activities, activities related to money laundering or illegal activities, or any fraudulent activities of any type.

I declare that I shall abide by all policies and procedures of ethics, confidentiality, information security, and behaviour at any of the JHS office as well as any of the client office I am deputed to.

During and after employment/consultancy with JHS, I shall not use any of the information that I get access to for my personal benefit, like insider trading activities, sharing of client confidential information for or without any consideration to any person.
